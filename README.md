# Your Name (Kimi no Na wa)

## Overview
**Your Name** (*Kimi no Na wa*) is a **Japanese animated romantic fantasy film** directed by **Makoto Shinkai** and released in **2016**.  
The movie beautifully blends **romance, drama, and supernatural elements** while exploring themes of connection and fate.

---

##  Main Characters
- **Taki Tachibana** – A high school boy living in Tokyo who dreams of something missing in his life.  
- **Mitsuha Miyamizu** – A high school girl from the rural town of Itomori who wishes to experience city life.  
- **Hitoha Miyamizu** – Mitsuha’s grandmother, who understands the spiritual bond that connects the two.  

---

## Plot Summary
Taki and Mitsuha mysteriously begin **switching bodies** across time and space.  
Through this strange connection, they develop a deep bond — even though they have **never met in person**.  
As they try to find each other, they uncover a heartbreaking truth that challenges both **fate and memory**.

> *“I wanted to tell you... wherever you are in the world, I’ll find you.”*
